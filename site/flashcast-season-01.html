On the winners

Names are missing on purpose.

Every week Flashcasters names its top market creators, and they earn the Magnet role. Those winners are announced by Discord name — and this archive stores X handles only, with no Discord identifiers anywhere in it. Guessing which X account sits behind a Discord name would mean linking two identities their owner never linked, so the chronicle keeps the weeks and leaves the people out.

If the announcements ever carry an X handle, the way the Community Spotlight already does, the winners land in Fermafia automatically — same list, same cards, second kind of recognition on the same page.
